Leadership Team

An alum of University of Waterloo’s co-op program, Ian Burgess, experienced firsthand the impact of apprenticeship-based learning and strong mentors early in his career. As both an academic and as a business leader, he has seen very-early-career students achieve extraordinary outcomes when given impactful work, really high expectations, and strong mentorship. Ian met Nicole Osborne at Validere, where she built the company’s talent function during a period of significant growth. Having helped several high-growth companies rapidly scale their teams, she understands the importance of mentor networks that pass on hard-won knowledge while reinforcing a culture of high standards.
