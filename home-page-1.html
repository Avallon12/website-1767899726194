WHat is ascendance Foundry?

Ascendance Foundry is built on two beliefs: 1) apprenticeship belongs at the centre of education, and 2) winning with AI demands the fastest workforce retraining in history. Our Ascendance Fellowship embeds a top student on your team after they have completed an intensive bootcamp focused on AI tools. The placement is supported by structured mentorship from seasoned founders, executives, and technical leaders. Every engagement includes co-learning: one of your employees gets access to the full program alongside the student—bootcamp, mentorship sessions, and project reviews. Our fellowships support a broad range of roles within companies, ranging from business functions, to analytics and software development. For larger initiatives, we build teams of Fellows and experts to deliver on projects ranging from strategic consulting to custom application development.

THE ASCENDANCE FELLOWSHIP

Launch your career with AI-First apprenticeships

Ascendance Fellowship is a fully paid, elite apprenticeship where you dive into real projects from day one. Fellows train with AI tools, deliver impact inside top companies, and learn from industry leaders. With expert mentorship, an ambitious peer network, and no tuition, it’s a launchpad to an accelerated, high-impact career.

Week 1: Intensive AI-Bootcamp

Intensive training in AI tools and development workflows. In addition to learning how to use AI to gain efficiency in business functions, every Fellow will build an app end to end.

Week 2-16+: Client Project Placement

Apply AI-native practices to deliver high-impact results, embedded in a real-world role at a client. Placements support a variety of roles including business functions, analytics, and software development.

Continuous Mentorship & Support

Access on-demand 1:1 mentorship throughout the term and learn from industry leaders through weekly workshops and group office hours with our Expert Network.
